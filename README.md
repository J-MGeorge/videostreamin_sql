# videostreamin_sql
SQL database
**On-Demand Streaming System**
- Holds information about video items, customers and various subscriptions.
- Offers three types of subscriptions: Basic, Advanced, and Unlimited.
  - Fixed monthly payment rate.
- The customer has an ID, email, name, payment card number, payment card expiration date, and address.
  - Will have subscription to one of three
  - Subscription has a start and possible end date
- Video items can be a movie or a TV series episode.
  - A title, genre, date, and duration.
